1. Physical-device demonstration
Please see the accompanying physical-iPhone screen recording. The PDF explains the workflows and includes a printable OCR sample. There are no app registration, login, account deletion or paid-feature flows.

2. Purpose and target audience
BZOGAK is a free personal reading app for physical books. Readers can save passages without marking a book, keep records and reflections, and listen to captured text. Optional AI helps explore their notes. The app is for the general public, not a restricted organization.

3. Setup and access
No app account, login or special file is required; your own printed text is sufficient.
- Bookshelf (책장): tap +, enter a book title and tap the checkmark. Search is optional. Open the book to add a reading record.
- Capture (캡처) > Underline (밑줄긋기): allow Camera, choose a book, capture a page and swipe the desired lines to save a passage. Add a memo and view the result in Bookshelf.
- Read Aloud (읽어주기): capture a page, tap the checkmark, then Play. No autoplay. Playback supports background use and screen locking. The default iPhone voice needs no third-party pack. Optional Korean Supertonic 3 requires a confirmed 401 MB download, then runs locally.
- Insights (인사이트): select passages, ask a question, generate and save. External AI also supports tags, OCR correction and review drafts. In Settings (gear) > 외부 AI 설정, select a provider/model, enter its API key and consent via 외부 AI 사용. Keys use Keychain. External AI is off by default and requires provider access; local capture, notes and speech need no AI key. Provider charges may apply.
- Community (커뮤니티): rankings, related blog search and Nearby (Location permission). Blog titles, snippets and thumbnails are read-only; originals open externally. No in-app publishing, comments, following or user-to-user messaging. Personal records are private. External blogs are third-party UGC; the app has no in-app article reporting or author-blocking controls.

4. External services and tools
Apple Vision: local OCR; Speech/AVFoundation: on-device voice memos and speech; Foundation Models: local OCR correction when available. ONNX Runtime runs Supertonic 3 locally; Hugging Face hosts the download. Our Vercel API uses Kakao Book/Local/Daum Blog, NAVER Blog Search, Aladin Book/Bestseller and Library Information Naru (data4library) loan-ranking APIs. Optional external AI uses OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or OpenRouter. No app authentication, advertising or payment service. Details are in the PDF.

5. Regional differences
There is no app-level regional feature switch. The interface is Korean. Nearby coverage is primarily South Korea, and rankings/blogs focus on Korean sources; results abroad may be empty. Apple model and voice availability depend on device, OS, language and region. External services depend on their own availability. Content is not identical worldwide.

6. Third-party material
This is not a regulated professional service. We supply no book texts or public book-page repository. Captured pages are processed locally. Users must respect rights when capturing/sharing. API metadata, covers and blog excerpts remain third-party material. The PDF identifies API sources and software/model licenses; original publishers retain their rights.
